About SIJI Consultancy

Founded in 2024 by Dr. Sridevi Tandley, SIJI Consultancy LLP is a strategic partner specializing in artificial intelligence, advanced analytics, and data strategy.

We operate at the unique intersection of government, institutions, and corporate entities. Our core objective is to harness the power of data to drive transparent, accountable, and value-driven decision-making that benefits both business and society.

We bridge the gap between theoretical research and real-world application. By combining deep academic rigor with hands-on industry expertise, we transform raw data into actionable intelligence and build AI-powered systems that deliver measurable impact.
